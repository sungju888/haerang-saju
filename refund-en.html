2. When refunds are not given

Because the reading is delivered instantly, we can't refund it once it has opened — for example for a change of mind, or because the reading wasn't what you hoped. We tell you this right above the pay button. Readings are an interpretation based on your birth data, for reference only.

3. When we refund in full

· You paid but the reading did not open
· You were charged twice for the same product (the second charge)
· A technical fault stops the reading you bought from showing, and we can't fix it after you contact us
· A minor paid without a parent or guardian's consent (on the guardian's request)

4. How to ask

Email us with your order number (shown on the payment screen and receipt), or the payment date and amount, and the reason. We refund within 3 business days of confirming.
