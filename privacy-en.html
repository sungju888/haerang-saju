Privacy policy

1. What we collect

· To calculate your chart: the name you want to be called (optional), gender, date of birth, time of birth (optional), place of birth
· To confirm payment: order number and payment confirmation details (card and payment details are handled by HitPay; we never receive them)
· Usage stats: a random visit ID and how you arrived (ad campaign name)

1b. If you sign in (optional)

If you sign in with Google or Kakao, we receive that account's unique ID, name and email address, and use them only to keep your purchased readings ("My readings") and to pre-fill your own birth details next time. We never receive your password. Email us to have this deleted. Signing in is never required to use any reading.

2. How we use it

Only to create and show your reading, confirm your payment, and give you a link to view it again.

3. How long we keep it

Unpaid entries are deleted automatically after 30 days. Paid readings are kept so you can view them again, and deleted on request. Payment records are kept as required by law.

4. Who processes it

· HitPay Payment Solutions (payments, Singapore) · Cloudflare (servers and storage). Your data may be processed outside your country.

5. Your rights

Email contact@soody.date to access or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. We reply within 30 days.
